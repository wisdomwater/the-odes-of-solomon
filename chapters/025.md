## 25 – Nothing Without Light

I was rescued from my bonds, and I fled to you, O my God. I did not linger beside the chains to understand every link, nor did I carry them with me as proof of what I had endured. When freedom opened before me, I ran toward the One whose hand had opened it. You were the right hand of my salvation and my helper.

Those who rose against me disappeared from my sight. Their voices had once seemed enormous, and their judgments had followed me even into solitude, but your face was with me. I learned that salvation is sometimes nothing more complicated than discovering where to look. When I turned toward your face, the faces that condemned me lost their power.

For I had been despised and rejected in the eyes of many. They weighed me and found me worthless, like lead discarded because it possessed no beauty or brilliance. Their measure entered me until I began to carry their judgment as though it were my own name. Yet you did not weigh me according to their scales.

From you came my strength and help.

You set a lamp at my right hand and another at my left. Light stood beside me on both sides, until there was nowhere I could turn and find myself abandoned to darkness. The road ahead was illuminated, but so was the ground beneath my feet; even the places behind me began to appear differently in your light. What I had remembered only as rejection became another place where your steadfast love had quietly accompanied me.

Then you clothed me with the covering of your Spirit. She drew near like a garment made of breath and light, and beneath her presence the old garments of skin fell away. I had worn so many coverings—fear, reputation, defense, the identities given to me by other people's eyes. Your Spirit removed them gently until I could stand before you without disguise.

Your right hand lifted me. You took sickness from me and strengthened what had grown weak through long captivity. I became strong in your truth, not because I learned to make myself hard, but because truth gave me somewhere firm to stand. Your righteousness made me holy by drawing me into what is whole.

Then the things I had feared began to fear the life awakening within me. Not because I had become threatening, but because chains cannot remain powerful before someone who knows they can be broken. Accusation loses its authority when the accused discovers another name. Darkness retreats when lamps have been lit on both sides.

I became the Lord's by the Lord's name.

This is the name deeper than the names others have given me, deeper even than the names I have given myself. I belong to you because you have called me yours. Your kindness justifies what contempt had condemned, and your love restores what rejection had diminished.

And now there is rest.

I do not have to prove that I was worth rescuing. I do not have to return to those who despised me and persuade them to see what you see. I can simply remain beneath the covering of your Spirit, held by the hand that lifted me.

Set your lamp again at my right, O Lord, and another at my left.

Let your light fall upon every road, every memory, every hidden corner of my heart, until I look around and discover that there is nowhere left without you.

And there is nothing without light.

Hallelujah!
