## 4 – The Unchanging Sanctuary

No one changes Your Holy Place, my God. Before there were earthly places to defend or borders to draw, before stone was raised upon stone and called sacred, Your sanctuary already was. What is older will not be changed by what is younger, and what was established in You cannot be overturned by anything that passes through time.

I have watched many things I thought permanent change. Places disappear, communities scatter, promises fail, and the structures in which I placed my confidence become fragile. Yet beneath all that shifts there remains something ancient and unmoved: Your heart given to Your faithful ones.

A single hour of Your faithfulness is more than all days and years. One moment rooted in You contains more life than years spent grasping for security elsewhere. For You do not become weary of goodness, and Your faithfulness does not diminish with the passing of generations.

You have placed Your seal upon creation. Your hosts bear it, and the elect archangels are clothed with it. Heaven itself seems to carry the mark of belonging, as though everything nearest to You has become radiant with the knowledge that it is Yours.

And You have given us partnership in intimate union.

Not because You need us. You are not incomplete without our praise, nor lonely without our attention. We need You, yet You draw us near freely, allowing creatures formed from dust to participate in the life from which stars and angels receive their being.

So sprinkle Your dew upon us.

Let it settle quietly upon every dry place within me. Open Your abundant fountains and let them pour forth milk and honey, nourishment sweeter and deeper than anything I can produce for myself. Where I have become barren through fear or striving, let Your goodness gather like morning dew until life begins again.

For there is no regret in You concerning what You have promised. You do not awaken tomorrow and withdraw the love You offered today. You knew the ending from the beginning, and still You gave.

What You gave, You gave freely.

This is difficult for the frightened heart to believe. I am accustomed to gifts that carry conditions and affection that can be withdrawn. Yet You were not surprised by my weakness when You welcomed me, nor did You discover some hidden failure afterward that made You reconsider Your generosity.

All was clear to You from the beginning.

So let me cease living as though Your heart must continually be won. Let me receive what You freely give and wear Your goodness without fear. Let Your seal become visible not as a mark of superiority, but as mercy, generosity, faithfulness, and love taking form within me.

Everything younger will pass away, but Your sanctuary remains.

When the places I have built can no longer shelter me, draw me into that older Holy Place that cannot be moved. Sprinkle Your dew upon me there, and let me rest beneath the promise You have never regretted giving.

For You, O Lord, have made all things.

Hallelujah.
