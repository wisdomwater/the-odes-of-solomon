## 28 – Beneath the Wings of the Spirit

As the wings of doves spread over their nestlings, so are the wings of the Spirit over my heart. I feel her nearness not as weight but as shelter, warm and living, guarding what is still tender within me. Like a young bird reaching its beak toward the one who feeds it, I turn toward the source of my life. I do not need to leave the nest to find what sustains me.

My heart delights beneath her wings. I rejoice like an unborn child stirring within its mother's womb, surrounded by a life it cannot yet comprehend. Before I have seen the world into which I am being born, I am already held. Before I understand God, I live within God.

I trusted, and therefore I rested. My rest did not come because danger disappeared, but because the One in whom I trusted proved trustworthy. I laid my head upon him while the sword remained in the world. The blade could come near my body, but it could not sever me from the One upon whom I rested.

For I had been established upon the imperishable bosom of his wings. Deathless life embraced me and kissed me. The Spirit within me is from that life, and she cannot die because she is life itself. Something deeper than my mortality has already been joined to what cannot perish.

Yet those who watched me could not see this hidden embrace. They saw persecution and assumed I had been abandoned. They watched me disappear beneath suffering and supposed I had been swallowed up. I seemed to them like one of the lost.

But what appeared to be my destruction became the place of my salvation.

I was hated, though I desired good for everyone. They surrounded me like maddened dogs, striking blindly at what they did not understand. Their bitterness came toward me with teeth exposed, inviting bitterness to awaken within me in return. I felt how easy it would be to become like what opposed me.

But I held waters in my right hand.

I endured their bitterness by my sweetness. I would not drink from the cup they offered simply because they pressed it against my lips. Hatred could wound me, but it did not have to reproduce itself within me. Their anger belonged to them; the living water entrusted to me belonged to another kingdom.

This is the freedom I ask you to preserve in me, O Most High. When I am misunderstood, keep me from becoming cruel. When I am rejected, keep me from learning rejection. When another's bitterness enters the room, remind me of the sweetness beneath the Spirit's wings.

For my deepest birth is from you. My life does not begin with the judgments spoken over me, nor can it end when others decide that I am lost. Before their accusations were formed, your thought held me. Before anyone remembered my name, I existed within your intention.

Your thought is beyond finding out, and your mind surpasses all wisdom. I cannot trace the boundaries of the life into which you have drawn me. I know only that beneath what can perish, I have touched the imperishable; beneath the noise of those who surround me, I hear the quiet movement of wings.

So I rest my head again.

Let the world misunderstand what it sees. Let it call lost what you have hidden close to your heart.

I will remain beneath the wings of the Spirit, holding living water in my hand, until every bitterness offered to me is answered by your sweetness.

Hallelujah!
