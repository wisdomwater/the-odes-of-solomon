## 1 – The Living Wreath

The Lord is upon my head like a wreath, and I will not be moved. I did not weave this wreath for myself or gather its branches through accomplishment. It was placed upon me as a gift, resting lightly where I had once carried heavier crowns.

For I have crowned myself with many things. I have worn the approval of others and trembled when their favor changed; I have worn achievement and discovered that every victory demanded another. Even goodness became a crown when I measured my worth by whether I had prayed enough, loved enough, or become the person I thought God desired me to be.

But these are dry wreaths.

Their leaves remain beautiful only for a season. Achievement can be lost, reputation can change, and even the image I have carefully constructed of myself eventually begins to crumble. But You live upon my head, and Your wreath does not dry.

Braided for me is the wreath of truth, and its branches bloom within me. I once imagined truth as something I could possess, gathering beliefs like stones and arranging them into walls against uncertainty. But Your truth is not a stone.

It is a branch.

It is alive, and when it comes from You, it does not remain in the mind. It sends its life downward into the heart, and buds appear upon branches I had assumed were dead. Slowly the truth I tried to possess begins possessing me.

I cannot know love without becoming more loving. I cannot receive mercy while refusing mercy to another. I cannot remain before Your goodness without something of that goodness taking shape within me as patience, kindness, forgiveness, courage, and compassion.

This is not like a dry wreath that blooms not. Sacred words can rest upon the lips while resentment remains untouched beneath them, and beliefs can be defended without producing a single living branch. But Your truth blooms, moving through the whole person until what I believe becomes what I love, and what I love becomes how I move through the world.

Then comes fruit.

Your fruits are full and ripe, and they are full of Your healing. This is how I recognize what grows from You: it does not leave the world more wounded than it found it. It does not require another to become smaller so that I may become greater.

Let that fruit ripen in me. Where anger has hardened, grow compassion; where fear has tightened, grow trust; where shame has taught me to hide, grow courage. Let Your branches stretch beyond every boundary I have drawn around love.

For the fruit is Yours, the healing is Yours, and the life is Yours. I need not manufacture them. I need only remain joined to the life that flows through the branch.

So let every dry crown fall away, one brittle leaf at a time. Let Your truth bloom within me until belief becomes love, and love ripens into healing.

And when nothing remains upon my head but You, let me discover that I have not been diminished.

I have become alive.
