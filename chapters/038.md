## 38 – The Chariot of Truth

I went up into the light of Truth as into a chariot, and Truth carried me. I did not know the whole road before I entered, nor was I shown every danger that waited beyond the horizon. I entrusted myself to the One who was and is Truth, and his light became movement beneath me. What I could not cross by understanding, I crossed by remaining with him.

There were gaping chasms and deep valleys, cliffs where one misplaced step seemed enough to undo me, and waves that rose higher than my courage. Yet Truth did not promise me a road without such places. He became my haven within them and brought me to the ladder of immortal life. Each step upward was another surrender to what was real.

I was in no danger because I walked with him. Error fled before his presence, for illusion cannot endure being fully seen. Whatever I did not know, Truth showed me—not all at once, but as my eyes became able to bear the seeing. He revealed even the poisons of Error, especially those poisons that taste sweet upon the tongue.

I saw what people mistake for Death's sweetness. I saw the glittering promises that intoxicate the heart: power without love, pleasure without communion, certainty without humility, belonging purchased by contempt for those outside. These things adorned themselves like a wedding feast. They promised union and gave only hunger.

Then I saw the bride and bridegroom of deception, mimicking the Beloved and his bride. They called everyone to their feast and poured the wine of intoxication freely. Those who drank surrendered their wisdom and their thoughts until they could no longer recognize what was happening within them. Having forgotten their own minds, they wandered outward again, spreading the destruction that had first entered them.

I asked Truth, Who are these?

These are Deceiver and Error, he told me.

And I understood that deception often succeeds because it resembles something worthy of desire. False love borrows the garments of love; domination dresses itself as strength; contempt calls itself righteousness. Yet Truth teaches me to know a thing by its fruit. Whatever destroys the mind, diminishes the neighbor, or empties love of mercy cannot belong to the wedding feast of God.

I rejoiced because Truth had gone with me. My foundations were laid by the hand of the Lord, and he planted me. After all the movement—the chariot, the valleys, the waves, the wandering paths—I became still enough to discover roots descending into the earth.

He set the root and watered it. He fixed it and blessed it, and the root struck deep while the branches rose toward heaven. I did not make myself grow. The same hand that rescued me from the chasm now tended the hidden soil around my life.

So let Truth carry me until I become planted in Truth. Let every false sweetness lose its taste, and every imitation fade before the presence of the Beloved. Water what you have planted, Lord, until my roots descend beyond the reach of deception and my branches become heavy with fruit.

Then let the tree stand quietly in your garden, bearing forever the blessing of your lips.

Hallelujah!
