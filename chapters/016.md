## 16 – The Craft of Praise

As the ploughshare belongs to the ploughman and the helm to the one who steers the ship, so praise has become my work. My occupation and my craft are the hymns of the Lord. His love has nourished my heart until its fruits bubble upward and reach my lips.

For my love is the Lord, and therefore I sing.

I do not sing because every day is easy or because sorrow has disappeared from the earth. I sing because beneath everything I encounter there remains a beauty deeper than circumstance. Praise turns my attention toward it until my heart remembers the One in whom it lives.

I open my mouth, and His Spirit speaks through me.

The song becomes larger than my own voice. It carries the glory of the Lord, the work of His hands and the labor of His fingers. The eye looks upon creation and sees His works; the ear becomes quiet enough to hear something of His thought.

He spread out the earth and settled the waters in the sea.

He stretched out the heavens and fixed the stars. He ordered creation and established its courses, and then He rested from His work. Yet what He made continues moving beneath His word.

The stars follow their paths. Waters rise and fall, seasons turn, seeds open beneath the soil, and creatures wake and sleep according to rhythms older than memory. Creation does not anxiously ask whether it has earned the right to exist; it receives its place and fulfills its task.

The storehouse of light is the sun, and the storehouse of darkness is the night.

Day brightens the earth, and night covers it again. Neither destroys the other. They meet and welcome one another at the horizon, and their meeting completes the beauty of God.

Teach me to receive my own days and nights this way.

There are seasons when Your presence seems bright as morning and others when I walk beneath darkness. Yet perhaps both can belong within a life held by You. The night need not mean abandonment when even darkness has its place beneath Your ordering word.

For there is nothing apart from the Lord.

Before anything came into being, You were. Before the first sunrise, before the first sea found its shore, before any voice learned to praise, all things rested within Your thought. The ages themselves unfold by Your speech and the intention of Your heart.

So let praise become my craft.

When morning comes, let me sing its brightness. When night arrives, let me trust the hidden stars. When I cannot understand the course of things, let me remember that creation moves within a wisdom deeper than my sight.

Open my mouth, O Lord.

Let Your Spirit breathe through me until my small voice joins the greater hymn—the sea, the stars, the turning earth, the meeting of darkness and light.

And let my life become one more note within the beauty of God.

Glory and honor to Your name.

Hallelujah.
