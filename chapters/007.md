## 7 – The Footprints of Light

My joy is the Lord, and my impulse is toward Him. Something within me turns toward Him as naturally as a child reaches toward love, without argument or calculation. This path is good because He Himself has made it possible, drawing me toward the One my heart was made to know.

Yet the One toward whom I turn is beyond everything I can comprehend. His greatness could overwhelm me, but He diminishes His greatness with kindness. He becomes like me so that I may receive Him, clothing Himself in a likeness I can recognize.

I trembled not when I saw Him.

He did not come to frighten me with His holiness. He came near enough to be touched, heard, and loved. The Invisible accepted visibility; the One beyond all form entered my form so that I would not turn away.

He knew me before I existed. Before my first thought awakened, He knew the paths I would walk, the good I would desire, the wounds I would carry, and the ways I would wander. Nothing I became surprised the One who called me into being.

And so He was merciful to me.

His mercy is not reluctant forgiveness offered after discovering what I am. Mercy was already present in the heart that created me. Knowing me completely, He still granted me the freedom to approach, to ask, and to receive from His abundance.

He has laid out a path toward knowledge, and upon it are the footprints of His light. They stretch from the beginning to the end, signs of a Presence that has always been moving toward creation. I follow those luminous footprints until the light rests in the Son.

There I recognize the One who made me.

I am not my own beginning. I did not summon myself from nothing or breathe life into my own lungs. My existence itself is a gift, and knowing God becomes also a remembering of where I came from.

His salvation reaches outward to take hold of everything.

Hatred is lifted from the earth, and envy is drowned beneath the rising knowledge of God. What cannot survive love begins to disappear—not because creation is destroyed, but because creation finally recognizes the One from whom it came.

Then the singers gather.

Those who see go out to meet Him with joy, carrying lyres of many tones. No two strings sound exactly alike, yet together they become one praise. Difference is not erased; it becomes harmony.

Let my heart become like the day.

Let whatever remains hidden in fear open beneath Your light. Let envy drown, let hatred lose its voice, and let every false belief that I must protect myself from another's flourishing disappear in the knowledge of Your abundance.

You have given a mouth to Your creation. Breath enters us, and breath becomes song. Trees rustle, waters move, creatures cry out, and human voices rise among them until nothing that breathes remains without praise.

So I will follow the footprints of Your light.

I will go out to meet You, not trembling before a stranger, but running toward the One who became like me so that I might know Him.

My joy is the Lord.

My heart is becoming day.

And my mouth will tell Your goodness.

Hallelujah.
