## 31 – The Rock Beneath the Breakers

The depths were dissolved before the Lord, and darkness was destroyed by his appearance. There are depths within me that seem ancient, places where fear has settled so deeply that I mistake it for part of myself. Yet when the Lord comes, he does not wrestle with the darkness as though it were his equal. He appears, and what seemed immovable begins to loosen and disappear.

Error goes astray before him. The tangled paths by which I wandered lose their power when truth rises like water and submerges them. Even Contempt, which once knew so many roads into the human heart—the road of shame, the road of accusation, the road by which I despise another or myself—can find no path to walk upon. The steadfast love of the Lord has flooded the old country.

And he opens his mouth.

What comes forth is not condemnation but steadfast love and joy. His voice carries a new song, something the world could not teach me because the world did not know how to sing it. I have heard the songs of striving and fear, of victory purchased through another's defeat, of worth measured against worth; but his song gathers the weary and calls them children.

He lifts his voice to the Most High and offers those who have come to life through his hands. I find myself among them, not standing at a distance as a servant awaiting judgment, but being presented as one who belongs. His hands have formed me again. What fear scattered, love gathers; what shame diminished, love raises and calls beloved.

Come forth, you who have been oppressed, and receive joy. Come from beneath the burdens you have carried so long that you have forgotten their weight. Gain your souls by his steadfast love, and take immortal life for yourselves—not as plunder seized from heaven, but as a gift already extended. Life is placed into open hands.

Yet the path of the Beloved passes through accusation. They condemned me when I stood up, though I had not been condemned, and they divided my spoils though nothing was owed to them. I felt the breakers strike: judgment, contempt, violence, the bitterness of those who believed they could diminish me by what they took.

But I endured and was silent.

I did not become the violence directed toward me. I did not allow bitterness to teach my heart its language. I stood unmoved like a solid rock beaten by the breakers, receiving wave after wave while remaining rooted somewhere deeper than their fury. My silence was not emptiness, nor was my humility defeat; beneath it lived a love they could not reach.

For I bore their bitterness that I might redeem rather than destroy. I would not answer contempt with contempt and so multiply its kingdom. I would inherit my people not by conquering them, but by refusing to abandon them. Even those who wounded me remained within the reach of the promise.

The breakers still rise and fall, but the rock remains.

Let your steadfast love become that rock within me, Lord. When accusation strikes, keep me from becoming accusation; when bitterness breaks against me, keep bitterness from entering my depths. Let darkness dissolve before your appearing until nothing remains in me but the new song.

And when the final wave withdraws, let me discover that beneath my feet there has always been immortal life.
