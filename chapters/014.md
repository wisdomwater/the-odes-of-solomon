## 14 – The Lyre of Your Spirit

As the eyes of a child toward a father, so are my eyes always toward You, O Lord. I look toward You because somewhere deeper than thought I know that my life comes from You. With You are my nourishment and my delight, the sweetness for which my hunger has always been reaching.

Do not turn Your love away from me. Do not take Your sweetness from me. When I lose the sense of Your nearness and the world seems emptied of Your presence, teach me to keep my eyes turned toward You.

Stretch out Your right hand and guide me until the end.

I do not need to see the whole road. I have often wanted certainty about where I am going, believing I could find peace if only the path were visible from beginning to end. But You offer something better than a map: Your hand.

Let me be beautiful before You because of Your glory.

I have tried to make myself beautiful before many eyes. I have adjusted myself to expectations, hidden what seemed unacceptable, and measured my worth by reflections in the faces around me. Yet the beauty You desire is not something painted upon me from outside.

Your glory makes me beautiful.

Let Your repose abide with me and the fruits of Your love grow within me. Let peace settle into the places where I have lived in restless striving. May love ripen there until what You have given inwardly becomes mercy, patience, gentleness, and joy offered outwardly.

Teach me the odes of Your truth so that I may produce fruits in You.

And open to me the lyre of Your Holy Spirit.

There are strings within me I do not know how to play. Some have been tightened by sorrow, others loosened by weariness; some have remained silent for years. Yet Your Spirit knows every note they were created to sound.

So place Your hand upon the instrument.

Let joy have its note and grief have its note. Let longing, wonder, repentance, gratitude, and hope each find their place within the song. Do not remove the darker tones, but bring them into harmony until even what has wounded me can become part of praise.

For Your mercy is abundant, and You know what I need before I can give it words. Nothing I bring exceeds Your ability to provide. Even my emptiness can become an open space into which Your music enters.

So my eyes remain toward You.

Your hand is before me.

Your Spirit moves across the strings.

Guide me until the end, O Lord, and let my life become the lyre You open with Your own hands.

Then let every note within me praise You.

Hallelujah.
