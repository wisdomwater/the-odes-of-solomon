## 12 – The River of Truth

He has filled me with utterances of truth so that I might speak. Truth came not as something I had captured, but as water seeking an opening. It flowed from my mouth, and my lips discovered its fruits.

The mouth of the Lord is truthful utterance and the opening of His light. When He speaks, something dawns. What was hidden becomes visible, and the mind awakens as a landscape awakens beneath the first light of morning.

Yet His utterance is greater than the words that carry it. It moves swiftly and sharply, passing beyond every boundary I build around it. Its course is endless, and it knows neither its descent nor its course.

I cannot trace the river back to its beginning.

I stand within its waters and receive what reaches me. I may cup my hands around a little of it, name what I have received, and offer it to another, but I cannot hold the river itself. Truth remains larger than every vessel into which I pour it.

And still it flows.

Through it the ages speak to one another. Voices long silent find speech again, and words spoken before my birth arrive like water from a distant spring. I drink from wisdom carried across generations and discover that the river passing through them now passes through me.

From this utterance come love and harmony.

This is how I recognize its truth. The speech of God does not merely make me certain; it draws me toward communion. It teaches those who hear it to speak to one another until strangers begin to recognize that the same breath has given them voice.

They knew the One who made them because they were in harmony.

How easily I have mistaken truth for the ability to win. I have sometimes wanted words sharp enough to defeat another, arguments strong enough to secure my place, certainty high enough to stand above those who see differently. But the truth flowing from the Most High bears another fruit.

Its truth is love.

And the Tabernacle of the utterance is the human one.

The Word does not remain distant. It seeks flesh, breath, hands, eyes, and a human voice. Divine speech desires to dwell among us until truth becomes embodied as mercy, courage, reconciliation, and love.

Make me such a dwelling, Lord.

Let Your truth pass through me without becoming stagnant in me. Keep me from building walls around the portion I have received and imagining that my small cup contains the whole river.

Let the words You give me create harmony rather than division. Let those who have been silenced recover their voices. Let every truth I speak bear the unmistakable fruit of love.

I do not know where the river began.

I cannot see where it will go.

But I hear it moving through the ages, and I open my mouth to its waters.

Let truth flow.

Let love be its fruit.

Hallelujah.
