## 34 – The Undivided Heart

No way is hard where the heart is single. The road may still climb, stones may still press beneath my feet, and I may not see where the path bends beyond the hill. Yet there is a different kind of difficulty born not from the road itself but from being divided within—one part of me moving toward God while another clings anxiously to what I fear to lose. When the heart becomes one, even the difficult road becomes simple: there is only the next faithful step.

Nor will any blow strike where thoughts are clear. The blows of life still come, but they no longer find the same confusion within me. Fear does not have to become despair, nor pain become bitterness, nor uncertainty become a thousand imagined futures. In the clarity of the illuminated mind, I can receive what comes without surrendering myself to it.

There is no storm in the depth of such a mind. Waves may churn upon the surface, winds may rise, and the sky above me may darken, but farther down there is stillness. I descend beneath the noise of my thoughts and discover a depth that the storm cannot reach. God is already there.

Surrounded on every side by light, nothing within the good person is divided. Light comes from above and rises from within; it shines upon what I welcome and what I would rather conceal. I no longer need to divide myself into the parts I offer to God and the parts I keep hidden from him. His light surrounds the whole of me, and in being wholly seen I begin to become whole.

The likeness of what is below is what is above. Heaven is not merely a distant country beyond the stars, waiting for me after I have escaped the earth. Its pattern is already pressing itself into creation, like light passing through a veil. What is eternal seeks expression here—in mercy given between human beings, in truth spoken without cruelty, in forgiveness stronger than vengeance, in love that refuses to possess.

Everything that truly exists is above, and what I call below is often the illusion of my unseeing. I mistake passing things for permanent things and shadows for substance. I build my identity from what can be taken from me, and then tremble because I know, somewhere within myself, how fragile these foundations are. Yet beneath the illusion remains what is real.

Steadfast Love has been revealed for my salvation.

This is the light by which the divided heart becomes one. I do not make myself whole by force, nor silence every storm through discipline. I turn toward the Love already surrounding me and allow what is scattered to be gathered. What I cannot reconcile within myself, I place gently into the light.

Trust, and live, and be saved.

Let my heart become single, O Lord. Gather my competing fears and desires into the simplicity of love. Illuminate the depths of my mind until I discover beneath every passing storm the stillness that has always been there.

And when I can no longer tell where the light above ends and the light within begins, let me rest there—undivided, surrounded on every side.

Hallelujah!
