## 18 – For Your Will Is Perfection

My heart was lifted up in the love of the Most High, and as it rose, it expanded. What had been cramped by fear grew spacious; what had curled inward around itself opened like a flower beneath the morning sun. Your love did not merely comfort me, O Lord—it enlarged me, until praise could breathe freely within my chest. Even my body seemed to awaken beneath the strength of your presence.

My limbs were strengthened that I might remain within your power. The infirmities that had clung to me began to loosen their hold, retreating before a life greater than themselves. They were not driven away by my strength, for I had exhausted myself trying to become strong. They departed according to your will, because your kingdom is firm, and whatever rests in you has found ground that cannot tremble.

Yet I look around me and see how much remains unfinished. I see those who are lacking, those wandering beneath burdens they do not understand, those whose works have wounded themselves and others. Do not deprive me of your utterance because the world has forgotten how to hear it. Do not withhold your perfection because we have filled our hands with broken things.

Let not the light be conquered by the darkness. Let truth never flee because falsehood has become loud, nor goodness hide itself because cruelty has learned to boast. There are hours when darkness appears immense simply because it surrounds the small flame I can see. But darkness has no flame of its own; it cannot create what it threatens to consume.

Stretch out your right hand, O Most High, and appoint victory for our salvation. Reach into every place where a human soul has become entangled—in fear, in hatred, in deception, in despair—and keep us from being held fast by our misfortunes. Receive us from every place, even those places from which we have forgotten the road home. Your hand is longer than our wandering.

For you are my God, and falsehood and death are not in your mouth. You do not speak death into what you have made, nor do you deceive your creatures concerning the purpose for which they were formed. Your will is perfection—not the brittle perfection of something afraid to be touched, but the wholeness in which every wound is healed and every scattered thing finds its proper place. What you desire is life made complete.

Vanity does not know you. Error does not know you. Ignorance rises like ocean spray, blown high by the wind, dazzling for a moment as though it were something immense. Yet the spray falls again into the sea, and all its boasting disappears.

How easily I have mistaken the spray for the ocean. How often I have given weight to what is weightless simply because it filled my sight. Empty things can appear enormous when they are near enough to the eyes, and falsehood can seem permanent when repeated from every side. But those who dwell in the mind of the Most High learn to see beyond what flashes for a moment.

Keep me there, within your mind, where my thoughts may become clear. Breathe into me again the breath you breathed at the beginning, so that when I speak, truth may ride upon that breath. Let me answer falsehood without becoming false, confront darkness without learning its darkness, and resist hatred without allowing hatred to take root in me. May your perfection shape not only what I oppose, but how I stand before it.

My heart rises again in the love of the Most High, and again it expands. There is room here for courage, room for mercy, room even for those still wandering with Error. Your light need not fear their darkness.

Breathe through me, O God, until only what is true remains upon my lips.

For your will is perfection.

Hallelujah.
