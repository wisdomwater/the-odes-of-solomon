## 24 – The End of Perishability

The dove fluttered down upon the Anointed One, because his head belonged to her. She rested upon him as though returning to a place she had always known, and her gentle voice became a psalm over him. Heaven did not remain silent when the Son stood among us. The Spirit cooed above him, tender and unmistakable, and creation heard her voice.

Yet what was gentle to him became terrifying to the powers that had made their home in darkness. The inhabitants were afraid, and the sojourners were shaken. Hidden things sensed that their hiding places would not remain hidden forever. Even the smallest creatures withdrew into their holes, as though the coming of the Spirit had disturbed something ancient beneath the surface of the world.

Then the depths were opened.

What had been covered was uncovered, and what had seemed sealed began to groan like a woman approaching the hour of birth. The depths themselves sought the Lord, trembling beneath the nearness of the One from whom nothing can remain concealed. Something was ending there, but something was also struggling to be born. Destruction and birth stood strangely beside one another.

The old depths could not sustain themselves forever. They had belonged to perishability from the beginning, and the Lord allowed what could not endure to reach its appointed end. Yet the end of their perishability was life. Beneath the terrifying collapse of what was passing away, the ancient thought of God was still moving toward renewal.

This gives me courage when the depths within me open. There are places I would rather keep covered—old fears, false identities, secret arrogance, the need to be right, the stories by which I protect myself from truth. When your Spirit descends, these things become restless. They know they cannot remain unchanged beneath the gaze of Love.

Do not let me mistake their trembling for the loss of myself.

What perishes beneath your presence is not the life you desire for me. Let falsehood perish. Let the thoughts that cannot answer truth dissolve when truth approaches them. Let every inner kingdom built upon pride discover that it has no foundation before you.

For arrogance lacks wisdom precisely because it cannot receive. It clenches itself around its own certainty and refuses the gift that would transform it. I have known this hardness in myself, the impulse to defend what I already believe rather than allow your light to show me something deeper. But your way is not revealed to the clenched heart.

You show your way and spread abroad your steadfast love. That love moves across the ruins after falsehood has fallen, not rejoicing in destruction but making room for life. Your holiness is not the cold purity of one who stands far from what is dying. You enter the depths yourself.

And there I find the Anointed One again.

The dove remains upon him. Her song continues while the hidden places open, while the old structures tremble, while perishability reaches its end. The Spirit who descended in gentleness is not frightened by the upheaval her coming awakens. She knows the thought by which all things were made from of old.

So descend upon me, Holy Spirit. Sing over Christ within me until every hidden depth hears your voice. Open what I have covered, dissolve what cannot live in truth, and carry every ending toward the life concealed within it.

Let the old perish without fear.

And when the depths have opened and their darkness has passed away, let me hear the dove still singing over the waters.

Hallelujah!
