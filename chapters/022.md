## 22 – Your Name Surrounded Me

You brought me down from on high, and you brought me up from the depths below. There was nowhere I descended where you had not already gone, no depth so hidden that your presence could not find me. When I rose, I did not rise alone, for the hand that accompanied me downward became the hand that lifted me. Above me and beneath me, I discovered the same God.

You gathered those who had been scattered in the places between. What had been divided you drew together, and what had stood against life you cast down. My enemies seemed powerful while I faced them alone, but before you they scattered like shadows before a rising flame. The things that claimed permanence could not endure the coming of your presence.

You gave me authority over bonds that I might loose them. You did not place chains in my hands so that I might become another jailer. The power you gave was the power to open, release, untie, and restore. In your kingdom, authority bends toward liberation.

Even the seven-headed dragon fell beneath your strength. Its many mouths had spoken fear, violence, accusation, and death, and whenever one head seemed defeated another rose in its place. Yet you led me beneath its terrible appearance to its roots. You set me there not merely to silence its voice for a moment, but to destroy the hidden offspring from which its cruelty continually grew.

And you were there.

You were there when I descended. You were there before the dragon and among the bonds, in the battle and in the silence that followed. In every place your name surrounded me. I thought I had entered territories abandoned by God, but when I opened my eyes I found your name written around the darkness like a boundary it could never cross.

Your right hand destroyed the venom of the evil one. The poison had entered deeply—into memory, desire, imagination, even into the ways we learned to wound one another. Yet your hand did not merely restrain the serpent; it drew the venom from the wound. Then you leveled a road where there had been no road, preparing a way for your faithful ones to walk home.

You called them even from the graves. You separated them from the dead and gathered bones long emptied of hope. Bone came to bone beneath your hands, and over them you stretched bodies; yet still they waited, silent and motionless. Then you gave them energy for life.

Breath entered what death had claimed.

Your way is imperishable. You brought your face into the age, even into the realm of destruction, and destruction could not destroy you. Instead, everything that could not endure your life began to dissolve. The old order cracked open beneath your feet so that creation itself might be renewed.

This is the mystery that steadies me: you do not fear ruined places. You enter them. You walk among graves, touch bones, confront dragons, break bonds, and descend into depths from which no one expects a return. Wherever death has written its final word, you bring another word.

And beneath everything stands your rock.

Upon it you build your kingdom—not a kingdom of graves and chains, but a dwelling place for the living. Gather what has been scattered in me, O Lord. Draw the venom from every wound, loosen every bond, and breathe into whatever has become motionless.

When I descend, let me find your name beneath me.

When I rise, let me find your name above me.

And when darkness surrounds me, let me awaken to the deeper truth:

Your name surrounds the darkness.

Hallelujah!
