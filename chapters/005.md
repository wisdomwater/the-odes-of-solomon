## 5 – I Will Not Fear

I give You thanks, O Lord, because I love You. Do not forsake me, Most High, for You are my hope. Freely I have received Your steadfast love, and I will live by what I have received.

There are times when fear gathers around me like an approaching army. I imagine what others might do, what might be taken from me, what judgment might fall, what carefully devised plan might succeed against me. My mind runs ahead into futures that have not happened and returns carrying their sorrows as though they were already mine.

But my life does not rest in their hands.

When my persecutors come, hide me beneath Your presence. Let darkness fall upon every intention that seeks harm, and let the path toward violence disappear beneath their feet. Not darkness that destroys them, Lord, but darkness that makes hatred lose its way.

Let every cruel design weaken before it reaches its purpose. Let deception collapse beneath its own weight, and let what was prepared for destruction come to nothing. For wickedness may make its preparations, but it cannot command the ending.

My hope is in the Lord, and I will not fear.

I say this while fear is still near. Courage does not require me to pretend that danger is unreal or that loss cannot wound me. It asks only that I remember there is something deeper than everything that threatens me.

The Lord is my salvation, and I will not fear.

You are again like a wreath upon my head, living where every lesser crown withers. I feel its branches resting upon me, reminding me that I belong to a Life no enemy gave and no enemy can remove. My security is not that nothing will ever shake, but that what is deepest in me has taken root in You.

Even if everything should be shaken, I will stand firm.

Let the familiar fall away. Let the structures I trusted reveal their weakness, and let the things I mistook for permanence pass like shadows. Beneath everything visible is the invisible communion by which I live.

And if everything visible should perish, I would not die.

For my life is more than the things I possess, the name I carry, the work of my hands, or the place I occupy in the world. There is a life hidden with You, nourished by steadfast love, and death itself cannot reach its source.

The Lord is with me.

And I am with Him.

This is the refuge beneath every other refuge: not escape from the world, but communion within it. Whatever comes, I need not meet it alone, for the One in whom I have placed my hope is nearer than the danger I fear.

So let the earth shake if it must. Let every scheme devised against love exhaust itself and disappear. I will remain beneath the living wreath, receiving again the steadfast love that was freely given.

You are with me.

I am with You.

And I will not fear.

Hallelujah.
