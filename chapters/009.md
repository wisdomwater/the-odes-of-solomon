## 9 – The Wreath of Victory

Open your ears, and I will speak to you. Give me your soul, and I will give you mine. Let there be no guarded distance between us, for what I bring is peace, and peace must be received with an open heart.

Your life is held within the will of the Lord. Before you knew to seek Him, His thought was already turned toward you, and within that thought was life without end. Your destination is not abandonment but immortality, not disappearance but fullness in the One who gave you breath.

Be enriched in God the Father.

There are riches I have gathered that cannot remain with me. Possessions pass from one hand to another, accomplishments fade from memory, and even the strength of the body eventually returns to the earth. But what I receive in God becomes part of the life awakening within me.

So receive the mind of the Most High. Let His thoughts reshape the thoughts that have kept you captive. Where fear whispers that everything will finally be lost, hear instead the intention of God: eternal life.

I proclaim peace to you so that you may not fall in the war.

There are wars fought around us, but there are also wars within. Fear struggles against trust, resentment against mercy, despair against hope. Yet the peace of God enters not as surrender to darkness, but as a strength by which darkness no longer determines what I become.

Those who know Him will not perish. Those who receive Him will not be ashamed.

And once again I see the wreath.

The eternal wreath is truth, and blessed are those who wear it. It is not made from leaves that dry with the season but from something enduring, a treasure of great price. Many have struggled for crowns, believing victory meant possessing what another must lose.

But this wreath comes differently.

Victory herself has taken it and given it to us.

I do not seize it from another head. I receive it from the hand of God. Divine victory does not require my neighbor's defeat, for the triumph of God is life overcoming death, truth overcoming falsehood, mercy overcoming cruelty, and love refusing to become hatred.

Put on the wreath in the steadfast covenant of the Lord.

Let its weight remind me to whom I belong. When fear tells me that I am losing, let me remember what victory looks like in the kingdom of God: to remain loving when hatred invites hatred, merciful when vengeance seems easier, faithful when everything visible trembles.

My name is written not because I conquered others, but because Your life conquered what was dying within me.

And Victory stands before me.

She does not accuse or demand. She desires that I be redeemed, holding out the wreath that I could never win for myself.

So I bow my head and receive it.

Let truth rest upon me.

Let peace remain within me.

Let Your life overcome everything in me that is not life.

And when the last battle has grown silent, let the wreath still be green.

Hallelujah.
