## 23 – The Decree of God

Joy belongs to the holy ones, and I put her on like a garment. Steadfast love belongs to those who trust in the Lord, and I receive her as something older than my remembering. Love surrounds me as though I had been clothed in him from the beginning. I walk in the knowledge of the Lord and discover no envy in his generosity, no reluctance in the hand that gives.

Then the thought of God becomes a Decree. His Will descends from on high, swift as an arrow released from a bow, carrying within it the purpose of the One who sent it. It does not wander uncertainly through the world, searching for somewhere to land. The force of divine intention is behind it.

Many hands reach for the Decree. They would seize it, possess it, break its seal, and make its words serve their own designs. Yet it slips from their fingers, and they tremble before the seal they cannot open. The authority upon it belongs to another kingdom.

I watch it pass beyond them and wonder where it will settle. Who will read what God has written? Who will hear it?

Then the Wheel receives the Decree.

It begins to move beneath the sign of the kingdom, carrying the Will of God through the world. Nothing can persuade it to abandon its course. Obstacles rise before it, powers gather against it, rivers interrupt its path and forests close around it, yet still the Wheel rolls onward.

It cuts through what resists the coming kingdom. It fills the rivers and uproots the forests, making a wide road where before there was none. Thrones and heads fall before it, and schemes carefully built through generations collapse beneath its turning. The Wheel does not ask permission from the powers that have claimed the earth.

I tremble before such an image, for I know there are forests within me as well. There are tangled places where I have hidden from truth, rivers across which I have built no bridge, small kingdoms where I have preferred my own will to yours. Let the Wheel pass through me also, O God. Make a wide path through everything that resists your love.

For the Decree gathers all places together. Nothing remains outside its summons, and no forgotten corner lies beyond the reach of the divine intention. At its head there appears a Head—the Son of Truth who comes from the Father Most High. What was hidden within the Decree now has a face.

He receives and inherits everything.

Before him the schemes of many are extinguished. Those who have made themselves servants of deception flee, and the works of persecution are erased like writing wiped from a tablet. Yet the true Decree remains, becoming itself a great tablet wholly written by the finger of God. What God has purposed cannot finally be rewritten by fear.

And I begin to understand why joy came first.

Joy is the garment of those who trust that beneath the confusion of the age there moves a deeper Will. Steadfast love belongs to those who believe that the Wheel is not turning toward emptiness, but toward the kingdom of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Love can stand without fear because the final authority belongs to Love.

So let your Decree come through every wilderness in me. Let it uproot what must be uprooted and open what has been closed. Let the Son of Truth rise at its head until every scattered place is gathered beneath his life.

And when I hear the Wheel approaching, let me not cling to what cannot remain.

Let me put on joy and stand beside the wide road your love has made.

Hallelujah!
