## 6 – The Unrestrained River

As the wind moves through the lyre and makes the strings speak, so the Spirit of the Lord moves through my limbs. I do not create the wind, nor can a string produce its own song. I make myself available to the breath, and love gives me a voice.

The Spirit moves where there had been silence. He passes through the tight places within me, through fear and guardedness, until even these begin to vibrate with His presence. What emerges is not merely speech about God, but the sound of His love passing through a human life.

For He destroys alienation.

I have lived as though separation were the deepest truth: God above and humanity below, myself here and my neighbor there, each of us enclosed within our own small boundaries. Yet the Spirit whispers of something older than our divisions. Everything is of the Lord, from the beginning to the end.

And the Lord desires to be known. His goodness does not hide itself behind locked doors or reserve its treasures for a favored few. Knowledge of Him multiplies because love longs to give itself away.

Then I see the stream.

At first it seems small enough to contain, a ribbon of living water moving quietly from the Most High. But the stream becomes a river, great and broad. It rises over its banks, covers the land, and carries even the Temple within its abundance.

The restrainers cannot restrain it.

They build their barriers and bring every skill they possess against the waters, but the river passes beyond them. No wall can persuade it to stop loving. No boundary can convince it that those beyond are unworthy of its water.

It goes over the face of the whole earth.

And the thirsty drink.

I see parched lips touched by water and faces awakening with surprise. Those who had nearly forgotten hope drink deeply, and the will that had fainted rises again. Souls that had drawn near to death discover life moving within them.

The water reaches fallen limbs and strengthens them. It enters tired bodies and teaches them again how to stand. It washes dust from clouded eyes until light returns and the world can be seen anew.

Blessed are those entrusted with this drink.

Let me never mistake the water for my possession. I am not its source, and I have no authority to decide who may thirst or who may drink. If You place this living water within my hands, let me carry it carefully to every parched place I encounter.

Let my words refresh rather than wound. Let my presence strengthen what has fallen rather than push it lower. Let those whose hope has grown faint find in me some small cup drawn from Your inexhaustible river.

For the water comes from the Most High.

And still it flows.

Move through me, Spirit, as wind through the strings of a lyre. Make even my weakness part of Your music. Break every barrier within me that would restrain what You desire to give.

Let the stream become a river.

Let the river cover everything.

And let every thirsty soul drink from the water that lives forever.
