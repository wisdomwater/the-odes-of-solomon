## 17 – The Gates That Were Closed

I was crowned by my God, and my wreath is living. It was not fashioned from leaves already dying, nor given to me as the reward of some victory I had won for myself. Life itself was woven around me, green and flowering, and what God placed upon me could not wither. I was justified by my Lord, and my salvation became something no passing hour could take away.

Then the bonds began to fall. With her hands she cut them from me—the cords of vanity, fear, falsehood, and all the empty things by which I had been held. I had worn them so long that I had mistaken them for parts of myself, but when they fell away, I discovered that beneath them another likeness had been waiting. I received the face of a new person, and I walked in it and was redeemed.

Those who had known the old face looked upon me with wonder. I seemed to them like a stranger, for freedom sometimes makes a person unfamiliar even to those who have known them longest. Yet I was not becoming someone foreign to myself; I was becoming the one whom the Most High had always known. He knew me beneath every covering, and by his kindness he raised my knowledge into the height of truth.

Truth went before me like a path appearing beneath my feet. I followed it, and it led me toward gates that had long been closed, gates strengthened by iron and guarded by fear. I put my hands upon them and opened them. The bars broke in pieces, and even the iron within me—the hard places I thought could never change—dissolved and melted before the presence of God.

Then I understood that I had not been freed merely so that I might walk alone into the open country. I went back toward the prisons. I went to all my prisoners to free them, because the freedom given by Love cannot be content while another remains bound. I would leave no one imprisoned, and neither would I leave untouched the desire to imprison others.

I carried no treasure that diminished when shared. I gave my knowledge without envy and my understanding through love, scattering what I had received as seed into human hearts. There the fruit began again: one life awakening another, one freed heart loosening the bonds of another, one flame passing its light without becoming smaller. What had grown in me became seed in them.

And they lived.

They gathered to me, and I discovered that they were not strangers standing outside me. They became my members, and I became their head, until the mystery of redemption was no longer the rescue of solitary souls but the gathering of a body. What had been divided was being joined; what had been scattered was coming home. Life moved through us as through branches sharing the same hidden root.

Now I stand again before the gates that were closed, but I no longer fear their iron. The One who crowned me has made my wreath living, and the One who broke my bonds has taught my hands the work of liberation. Let every bar within me melt before your love, O Most High, until nothing remains in me that binds another.

Make me a gate through which your life may pass.

And where I find a prison, let me enter carrying the freedom with which you first set me free.

Praise to you, our Leader, our Anointed Master.

Hallelujah.
