## 21 – Clothed with Light

My arms I lifted up because of the compassion of the Lord. They rose almost before I knew what prayer they meant to offer, as though my body understood gratitude before my lips could speak it. The bonds that had held me were no longer there; they had fallen away beneath hands more merciful than mine. I lifted empty arms toward the One who had emptied them of their chains.

My Helper lifted me because of his compassion and his salvation. I had imagined that I must somehow climb toward God, pulling myself upward through effort, understanding, and purity, but I discovered myself being carried. His compassion came beneath me where my strength had failed. Salvation was not the reward at the summit of my ascent, but the arms that raised me from the ground.

And I put off darkness and clothed myself with light. Darkness had rested upon me so long that I had almost mistaken it for my own skin, but now it slipped from my shoulders like an old garment. Light touched the places I had hidden, not to shame them, but to heal them. I stood uncovered for only a moment before the mercy of God became my clothing.

Then my soul acquired limbs free from pain, sickness, and suffering. What had been cramped within me began to stretch; what had forgotten how to move remembered its freedom. I felt as though some deeper body had awakened, one made for a country I had only glimpsed from far away. I could stand where I had fallen, reach where I had withdrawn, and walk where fear had once forbidden me to go.

The thought of the Lord became medicine and helper to me. His thought entered the wounds beneath my wounds, deeper than the stories I had told myself about who I was and what could never change. He did not merely soothe the surface. His presence joined itself to me in intimate union, and his imperishable life began to move through everything in me that feared decay.

What medicine is this, that the Physician gives himself?

I was not healed by being kept at a distance from God, as though holiness required my separation. I was healed through partnership, through communion, through a life shared. His imperishability touched my frailty, and my frailty did not contaminate him; instead, his life entered me. The nearer I came, the more alive I became.

And I was lifted up in his light and passed before his face. I did not turn away. The face I had feared would expose everything became the face before which I finally had nothing to hide. I drew near to him praising and thanking him, because I discovered that to be fully seen by Love is not destruction but freedom.

Then my heart overflowed and was found in my mouth. Praise rose without being summoned, like water reaching the rim of a vessel already filled beyond its measure. Something luminous dawned upon my lips, and even my face began to bear the joy that had awakened within me. What God had done secretly in my depths could no longer remain hidden.

So I lift my arms again, O Lord. Let every remaining bond fall from them, and clothe every shadow in me with your light. Be medicine where I am wounded, strength where I am weak, and intimate companion where I have imagined myself alone.

Join your imperishable life to mine until your dawn rises from within me.

And when my heart can hold no more of your goodness, let the light spill from my lips and shine upon my face.

Hallelujah!
