## 3 – The Lover and the Beloved

I cover my limbs with light, and His limbs are with me. I cling to Him, and He loves me. I no longer know where the light resting upon me ends and the light coming from Him begins, for love has drawn near enough to erase the distance I once imagined between us.

I could not have known how to love the Lord if He had not loved me first. I thought my longing began within myself, as though I had awakened one morning and decided to seek God. But beneath my seeking was His seeking, beneath my desire was His desire, and beneath every movement of my heart toward Him was the quiet movement of His heart toward me.

Who can understand love except one who is loved?

Love cannot be learned from a distance. I may speak of it, study it, and wonder at it, but only when I allow myself to be loved does its mystery open. The knowledge of God enters not merely as an idea but as warmth entering cold limbs, until what had been guarded begins to soften.

I love the Beloved, and my soul loves Him. Where His rest is, there too am I. I have spent so long searching for rest in places that could not hold me, yet now I discover that rest is not merely the absence of struggle; it is the presence of the One in whom my soul recognizes its home.

Nor will I be a stranger there. With the Lord Most High, the Merciful, there is no envy, no guarded doorway, no reluctant welcome. God does not fear my nearness or diminish when I draw close, for divine love does not protect itself from those who come to receive it.

I have been united with the Most High because the Lover has found the beloved. I cling to the Son, and in loving the Son I too become a son. What belongs to Him begins to become mine—not by possession, but by participation, as a branch shares the life of the vine and becomes alive with a life greater than itself.

He who cleaves to the Immortal becomes immortal. The one who delights in Life becomes living. I do not seize eternal life as a treasure held apart from God; I enter Life by entering more deeply into Him.

This is the Spirit of the Lord, faithful and true, teaching me His ways from within. The Spirit teaches my heart to recognize what resembles God: mercy instead of domination, communion instead of separation, generosity instead of envy, love instead of fear.

So make me wise and knowing. Keep me watchful for every place where Love approaches disguised as invitation, forgiveness, beauty, another human face, or the quiet desire to come home. Do not let me search far away for the One whose limbs are already with me.

I cling to You, and You love me.

Let Your light cover my limbs until I become luminous with what I have received. Let Your life enter mine until I become living, and let me rest where You rest.

The Lover has found the beloved.

And I am no longer a stranger.

Hallelujah.
