## 32 – The Light Within

Joy rises from the hearts of the blessed, not as something summoned from the world around them, but as something discovered within. I have searched for joy among changing things and have found that everything I grasp eventually changes in my hands. Yet beneath these movements there is another joy, quiet and deep, flowing from the One who dwells within me.

There is light from him who dwells in us. It does not shine only from some distant heaven, calling me toward a place I have not yet reached. The Holy One has made the human heart his dwelling, and therefore his light can appear in the very place where I have known darkness. I close my eyes and become still, and discover that I am not alone within myself.

This light does not require me to deny the shadows. Grief may remain grief, uncertainty may remain uncertainty, and the wounds I carry do not disappear merely because I have entered the presence of God. Yet darkness is no longer the deepest thing in me. Beneath everything I fear losing is the One who cannot be lost.

And from this indwelling presence comes an utterance—the utterance of the Truth who is self-existent. Before I spoke his name, he was. Before anyone praised him, he lacked nothing; before creation awakened beneath his gaze, he rested in the fullness of his own life. His truth does not become true because I believe it, nor does it cease to be true when I cannot perceive it.

How often I have tried to make myself secure by arranging everything around me. I have gathered explanations, accomplishments, possessions, and the approval of others, hoping they might form a foundation beneath my feet. But everything created rests upon something else, and every certainty I manufacture eventually reveals its fragility. Only the Truth who is self-existent rests upon no other ground.

And yet this uncreated Truth chooses to dwell in me.

I feel the wonder of it more than I understand it. The One who needs nothing makes room within himself for communion, and the One whom nothing can contain makes his home within the human heart. His life does not erase mine but strengthens it, as fire fills iron with its heat without destroying the iron, until what was cold begins to glow.

He was strengthened by the holy power of the Most High, and he is unshaken forever and ever. Therefore I need not make myself unshakable. I may tremble and still be held; I may question and still remain within the light. My strength is not the absence of weakness but the presence of the One who cannot be moved.

Let the noise within me grow quiet, then, until I hear the utterance beneath all my other words. Let borrowed lights fade until I recognize the light that has been shining from within. Let the joy of the blessed rise gently from the hidden chambers of my heart.

For beneath everything that changes, the Truth remains.

And in the stillness, I discover that the One who is unshaken has made his dwelling in me.

Hallelujah!
