## 41 – Remembered by the Father

All the children of the Lord will praise him, for they have discovered the firmness of his faithfulness. We are known by him before we know how to name him, held before our hands learn how to reach. His steadfast love is the place in which we live, and through his Anointed One we receive life. So let us sing within that love, as children who have awakened to find themselves already at home.

A great day has shone upon us. Its light does not belong to the sun alone, for it reaches places no earthly morning can enter. It illuminates the hidden rooms of the heart and reveals the goodness that has been surrounding us even in darkness. Wonderful is the One who gives us of his own glory and does not guard his brightness from us.

Let us agree together in the name of the Lord. Let our faces shine in his light, and let our minds study his Love by night and by day. I read this Love slowly, almost beneath my breath, discovering its words in mercy received and mercy given, in wounds being healed, in strangers becoming neighbors, in every quiet movement by which life is restored. Love becomes a sacred text written across the world.

Those who see me will be amazed, because I am of another kind. Yet I have not become less human; I am becoming human in another way. The old patterns of fear and rivalry no longer need to tell me who I am. Another life has entered mine, and its likeness is slowly appearing upon my face.

For the Father of Truth remembered me.

There is rest in those words. I have feared being forgotten—lost among the multitude, overlooked in the vastness of creation, or hidden even from myself beneath everything I have become. But the Father remembered me because I was already present in his heart. His wealth conceived me, and I arose within the thought of his love.

His Speech has accompanied me along the whole road. She has been with us in all our way, making alive and refusing to reject our souls. Before I recognized the Presence walking beside me, the Presence had already recognized me. Before I understood the path, the Word was quietly becoming light beneath my feet.

Then the Son of the Most High appeared in the fullness of his Father—the humbled One who was exalted, the Anointed One who is one with Truth. From the ancient Speech light dawned, though that light had lived in the Father before the foundation of the age. What appeared among us in time had its roots in eternity.

And this eternal light came to make souls live.

I feel its purpose moving through me: not condemnation, not abandonment, but life. The Messiah makes alive by the truth of his name, gathering what has become scattered and awakening what has fallen asleep. The remembrance of God is creative; when he remembers me, I remember who I am.

So a new hymn rises from those who love him. It is sung by lives becoming luminous, by minds learning Love, by souls discovering that they were never absent from the heart of God.

Remember me, Father of Truth, whenever I forget myself.

Let your ancient Speech dawn within me until my face shines with the life of your Son.

And let me live as one who has always been remembered.

Hallelujah!
