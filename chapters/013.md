## 13 – The Mirror of the Lord

Look, the Lord is our mirror.

I open my eyes and look into Him, and there I begin to learn the nature of my own face. I have searched for myself in many other mirrors, hoping they might tell me who I am. Some praised me and some accused me, but none could show me as clearly as the face of God.

The world holds up countless mirrors. I see myself reflected in the approval of others, in my accomplishments and failures, in comparisons, memories, and judgments. Each reflection changes with the light, and if I stare too long I begin to mistake these shifting images for myself.

But the Lord is my mirror.

When I look into Him, I do not find the face I feared He would show me. I expected perhaps an inventory of everything unfinished, every failure enlarged beneath the brightness of His holiness. Instead, His holiness reveals something deeper than my shame.

I see a creature loved into being.

I see a face God desired before it had learned to hide. Beneath the masks I have worn and the identities I have constructed is something His hands still recognize. His Spirit teaches me to see myself not through illusion or pride, but through the truth of belonging.

So I wipe the eyeshadow from my face.

I remove what I have painted there to become acceptable. I lay aside the disguises fashioned from achievement, certainty, reputation, and the need to appear stronger than I am. Before God I do not need to improve the reflection before allowing myself to be seen.

Love His holiness, and put it on instead.

His holiness is not another mask. It is the restoration of the face beneath the masks. As I gaze upon Him, something of what I behold begins quietly to appear in me.

Mercy teaches my eyes to look differently upon another. Truth loosens the need to pretend. Compassion softens what fear had hardened, and love becomes visible where self-protection once shaped my expression.

Perhaps this is how the face becomes without blemish—not by frantic polishing, but by remaining before the true Mirror. I become what I behold because His Spirit is alive within me. His holiness is not painted upon the surface; it rises slowly from within.

So keep my eyes open, Lord.

When I am tempted to search for myself in lesser mirrors, turn my face again toward You. When shame distorts what I see, let Your Spirit clear my vision.

Let me look until every false image fades.

Let me look until I recognize the face You have always known.

Let me look until Your holiness becomes my beauty.

For You are my mirror, and in Your face I am learning my own.

Hallelujah.
