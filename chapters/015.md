## 15 – Awakened by the Sun

As the sun is joy to those who wait for the day, so is my joy the Lord. I had lived long enough among shadows to mistake them for the shape of the world. Then His rays reached me, and morning entered my face.

The Lord is my Sun.

His light did not merely reveal what surrounded me; it awakened something within me capable of seeing. By Him I acquired eyes and beheld His holy day. I discovered that awakening is itself a gift, for even the eyes with which I seek God are opened by God.

He gave me ears, and I heard His truth.

There had always been many voices around me, some promising life and others teaching fear. I followed paths because they were familiar and believed things because I had heard them repeated. But beneath all that noise came a quieter voice, carrying the unmistakable sound of life.

So I forsook the way of Error and walked toward Him.

I did not find a guarded gate. I found generosity. Salvation came without envy, as sunlight falls upon a field without asking which blade of grass has earned its warmth.

According to His generosity He gave to me, and according to the magnificence of His beauty He made me. The longer I remained beneath His light, the more His beauty awakened beauty within me. What I received began to shape what I became.

I put on imperishability by His name.

I took off perishability by His steadfast love, like an old garment whose threads had begun to unravel. I had clothed myself in things destined to pass—possessions, accomplishments, reputation, even the body I could not keep forever. Beneath them all, God clothed me with a life that death could not consume.

Then death was destroyed before my face.

I still see mortality around me. Leaves fall, bodies weaken, beloved things pass beyond my reach. Yet death no longer appears as the final horizon, because beyond it I have seen the rising of another day.

Deathless life rises in the Lord's land.

It rises like morning after a night that seemed endless, touching graves and forgotten places with light. Immortality becomes known not as something hoarded by a fortunate few, but as a gift given without loss to all who receive. The Sun is not diminished because many stand beneath His rays.

So awaken me again, O Lord.

Where darkness has gathered upon my face, let Your light reach me. Where fear has taught me to expect only endings, give me eyes for Your holy day.

Let me take off what perishes.

Let me put on Your life.

And when the night seems deepest, turn my face toward the horizon, where deathless life is already rising.

For You are my Sun.

And morning has come.

Hallelujah.
