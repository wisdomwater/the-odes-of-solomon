## 29 – Out of the Mouth of Death

The Lord is my hope, and in him I will not be ashamed. My hope does not rest upon my ability to preserve myself, nor upon the promise that no darkness will ever find me. It rests in the One who made me by his glory and gave to me from his goodness. His love lifted me before I knew how to rise.

By his majestic beauty he exalted me. I had looked toward so many other heights, imagining that I would become whole if only I could climb far enough above weakness, failure, and fear. Yet the height to which God raised me was not a place of superiority. It was the height from which I could finally see that I had always been held within his steadfast love.

For I had descended into the depths of Sheol. I knew the country where light seems distant and every road appears to lead farther downward. Death opened its mouth as though it could swallow me and erase even the memory that I had lived. But the Lord reached into the mouth of Death and drew me out.

I felt the grip release.

The darkness that had seemed infinite suddenly had an edge, and beyond that edge stood the Anointed One. I had trusted in him without seeing where the trust would lead, and then he appeared to me—the One who is Lord. He showed me his sign and led me by his light. I did not escape the depths by learning their darkness, but by following a light they could not extinguish.

Then he placed the rod of his power in my hand. Yet his power did not teach me to become what had oppressed me. I was not rescued from domination so that I might dominate. His authority exposed the schemes by which power makes itself great at the expense of others and brought their false greatness low.

He taught me to wage war by his speech.

This warfare is strange to the kingdoms of the world. Its weapon is truth, and its victory belongs to God. I do not need to answer deception with deception, cruelty with cruelty, or force with the desire for greater force. The word of the Lord enters what appears immovable and reveals how little substance falsehood truly possesses.

My enemy became like chaff carried by the wind.

How heavy fear had seemed when I carried it within myself. How permanent the powers of darkness appeared when I stood beneath their shadow. Yet before the speech of God, what I had mistaken for stone became straw. The wind rose, and what had threatened to define my life scattered across the open field.

So I will not be ashamed of my hope. I have seen where it reaches. It reaches into Sheol, into failure, into every place where I imagine myself beyond recovery. It reaches even into the mouth of Death.

Magnify your servant, O Most High, not by making me greater than others, but by making your life greater within me. Put your rod in my hand without allowing my hand to become cruel. Put your speech upon my lips without allowing truth to become another weapon of pride.

And when I descend again into some depth where hope seems foolish, let the Anointed One appear.

Let his sign rise before me.

Let his light find me even there.

And from the mouth of Death, draw me once more into life.

Hallelujah!
