## 11 – The Land That Laughs

The Most High circumcised my heart by His Holy Spirit, and its flower appeared. What had been hidden beneath coverings was laid bare before Him, not for shame, but for healing. Into that opened place He poured His love, and steadfast love sprang up and began to bear fruit.

His cutting became my salvation. There were things I had carried so long that I mistook them for myself—fears, vanities, old defenses, desires that promised life but left me empty. The Spirit stripped them away so that the heart beneath them could breathe.

Then I ran upon the path of peace, along the way of truth. He established my feet upon the rock, and from the fountain of the Lord speaking waters reached my lips. I bent down and drank.

I drank until I became drunk with living water.

Yet this was no drunkenness of forgetfulness. My eyes became clearer, not dimmer. I saw the vanities I had chased and left them behind, casting folly to the ground like an old garment I no longer needed to wear.

And the Lord clothed me anew.

He wrapped me in light and gave me rest in imperishability. I had spent so much strength trying to make myself secure, yet now I rested in a life I had not created and could not sustain by my own effort. Beneath His light, something within me began to grow.

I became like land that flourishes and laughs in its fruits.

The Lord was the Sun shining upon my face. My eyes brightened, dew settled upon me, and my breath rejoiced in His sweet fragrance. What had once felt barren became a garden awakening beneath morning light.

Then He carried me into His paradise.

I expected perhaps a small enclosed garden, guarded carefully against those who did not belong. Instead I found plentiful room. Nothing lay fallow there; everywhere life was rising, branches stretching, roots drinking, fruit ripening beneath the generosity of God.

Blessed are those planted in Your land.

They pass from darkness into light and grow among Your trees. Bitterness does not have the final word over them, for when they are planted in Your soil even bitter roots can be transformed. Your sweet gentleness enters what was wounded and teaches it another way to grow.

Plant me there, Lord.

Where bitterness remains in me, let Your gentleness reach the root. Where the soil has hardened, send Your living water. Where I have believed nothing good could grow again, shine upon that place like the sun.

Let my life become land that laughs in its fruits.

Not fruit produced through anxious striving, but the abundance of a heart opened by Your Spirit, watered from Your fountain, and warmed beneath Your face.

There is plentiful room in Your paradise.

So plant us deeply.

Let nothing lie fallow.

And let the whole land awaken beneath the eternal delight of Your light.

Hallelujah.
