## 10 – A Captivity for Freedom

The Lord directed my mouth by His speech and opened my heart by His light. Before I could speak peace, peace had to take root within me. Before I could carry light into the world, my own heart had to be opened enough to receive it.

Then He caused His deathless life to dwell in me.

I feel that life moving beneath everything mortal, a current untouched by decay. It is not merely the promise that I will live someday beyond death. Eternal life has already entered me, and from its abundance comes the fruit of peace.

So I speak to those who desire to come to Him.

I do not call them into another prison. Too many voices have offered belonging at the price of freedom, demanding fear, conformity, or submission to those who desire power. But the captivity of God is a strange captivity: I am captured by the One who sets me free.

I lead captive a good captivity for freedom.

Love takes hold of me, and the chains I once accepted begin to fall away. Fear loses its claim upon me. Shame no longer tells me who I am, and resentment no longer determines whom I am able to love.

I became strong and took the age captive.

The powers that seemed immovable are not eternal. Every age builds its kingdoms and declares them permanent; every generation creates measures of worth and teaches its children to bow before them. But the deathless life of God passes through them all.

And the scattered are gathered.

Those divided by distance, injury, suspicion, and sin begin to find one another again. I do not gather them because they have never wounded me. Some have sinned against me, yet I will not allow their sin to enter me and become my own.

I will not be defiled by hatred.

Their wrong will not teach me to wrong them in return. Their bitterness will not decide what kind of person I become. The peace dwelling within me is stronger than the wound, and forgiveness becomes another form of freedom.

Then the footprints of light are laid upon their hearts.

I have seen these footprints before, stretching from the beginning toward the end. Now they appear within human hearts, marking a path home. Light has walked here, and those who discover its traces begin to follow.

They live by my way and are redeemed.

Let that way be visible in me, Lord. Direct my mouth with Your speech when I am tempted to wound. Open my heart with Your light when pain urges me to close it.

Gather what has been scattered.

Free what has been captive.

Let Your deathless life dwell so deeply within me that even those who have wounded me encounter not the darkness they gave, but the peace You planted there.

Take me captive, O Love.

And in that captivity, make me free.

Hallelujah.
