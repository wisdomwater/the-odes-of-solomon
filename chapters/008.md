## 8 – I Know My Own

Open your hearts to exultation in the Lord, and let love rise from the heart to the lips. What begins secretly within you will not remain hidden forever. Love becomes speech, mercy becomes action, and the life received from God ripens into holy fruit.

Rise and stand upright, you who were brought low. You who learned silence, speak, for your mouth has been opened. You who were despised, lift your head, for the right hand of the Lord is beside you, and peace was prepared for you before your war ever began.

There is something deeper than the struggle.

Before the wound was peace. Before rejection was belonging. Before fear taught me to hide, I was already known by the One who fashioned me.

And now I hear His voice.

I do not turn away My face from My own, because I know them.

Before they came into being, I understood them. Before they could succeed or fail, before they could become beautiful or broken in their own eyes, I knew them. I fashioned their limbs and formed their minds and hearts, and they are Mine.

How difficult it is to hide when I hear this voice. I have imagined that if everything within me were exposed, some part might cause God to turn His face away. Yet He knew before I knew myself, and still He brought me into being.

I was well pleased in them, He says, and I am not ashamed of them.

Let those words descend into every hidden place. God is not ashamed of His work. He does not look upon the creature He formed with embarrassment or regret, as though discovering too late what His own hands had made.

He prepares His own breast and gives holy milk that His children may drink and live. The image is tender beyond measure: the Most High nourishing what He has created from His own life. I need not steal life from the world or prove that I deserve to exist; I open my mouth and receive.

Beloved in Beloved.

Kept within the One who lives.

Redeemed within the One who was redeemed.

This is where I abide. Not at the edge of divine affection, afraid that one failure will cast me outside, but within a love that knew me before my beginning. His name is with me, and His righteousness goes before me.

So I will seek deeply and remain in the love of the Lord. When shame tells me to lower my face, I will remember the face that does not turn away. When fear tells me I am alone, I will remember the right hand beside me.

You knew me before I existed.

You fashioned my heart.

You are not ashamed of me.

So let me rise from the place where I was brought low and stand within Your love, beloved in Beloved, held within the One who lives.

And there, beneath the face that never turns away, let me become imperishable.

Hallelujah.
