Before Christian faith was expressed through centuries of creeds, commentaries, and theological systems, it was sung.

The *Odes of Solomon* are among the earliest surviving Christian hymns—a remarkable collection filled with living water, radiant light, honey and milk, flowering trees, raging rivers, divine Wisdom, the indwelling Spirit, and Christ descending into the depths of Death itself.

This contemplative retelling invites these ancient songs to breathe again.

Each Ode has been expanded into a spacious meditation that enters its imagery rather than explaining it from a distance. Here salvation is something experienced: the thirsty drink, the lost are gathered, the Spirit gives birth within the human heart, steadfast love descends into darkness, and the life of Christ becomes life within us.

At times the worshiper speaks to Christ. At other times Christ seems to speak through the worshiper. Their voices mingle in a vision of faith centered not merely on believing in God, but on participating in divine life.

For readers drawn to early Christianity, Christian mysticism, contemplative prayer, or the forgotten treasures of the ancient Church, *Odes of Solomon* offers an invitation to listen.

Come to the living fountain.

Stand within the light.

And discover an ancient song that is still being sung.
